BookSim Notes

A chapter-by-chapter study of Principles and Practices of Interconnection Networks (Dally & Towles, 2004). All 25 chapters retain the book's section numbering, titles and order, with printed-page references, fixed-version BookSim2 source analysis and editable academic diagrams. Additional Mesh smoke experiments are documented separately from the book's examples.

原书目录、小节页码、源码路线与图片来源 · 补充实验与实际结果

Original Declaration

The Chinese explanations and newly drawn teaching diagrams are study notes by Herbert. Chapter organization, theories and historical case studies derive from Dally and Towles; external explanations are attributed where used. Please attribute original commentary to this site.
