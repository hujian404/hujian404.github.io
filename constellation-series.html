Chipyard Constellation

A deep dive into Constellation — the open-source, SoC-capable Network-on-Chip generator from UC Berkeley, integrated into the Chipyard agile SoC framework. Rather than stopping at "how to run Constellation", this series works from the paper, through the design abstractions and GitHub source, down to the Chisel/RTL implementation, and finally into NoC performance experiments and secondary development — building a systematic understanding of a modern, highly parameterized NoC generator. The series follows a five-stage roadmap: Understanding Constellation → NoC Generator → Router & Protocol → Evaluation → Research.
