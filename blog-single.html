Hello, World — Welcome to My Blog

by Herbert / Intro

Hi, I'm Herbert — an IC design engineer. My research focuses on GPU architecture and the modeling and implementation of Network-on-Chip (NoC). I started this blog to write down what I learn about chip design and computer architecture, and to share notes that might be useful to others working in the same area.

This first post is just a hello. I'll be adding more about GPU microarchitecture, NoC topology and routing, RTL design, and performance modeling as I go. Thanks for stopping by.
